Services

What I build

Four areas that usually turn up in the same project: a website needs somewhere to run and a form that actually delivers, and an app needs something behind it.

  1. 01

    Websites

    Responsive business sites with a clear structure, fast pages and accessible markup. WordPress with a custom theme when the content has to be edited by its owner, hand-built when it does not.

  2. 02

    Android applications

    Native Android in Kotlin, taken to production: navigation, background work and offline behaviour, plus the Play release, the store listing and the data-safety declarations that come with it.

  3. 03

    Backend and integrations

    APIs, databases, sign-in and the third-party services a site or an app depends on: storage, backups and the server-side parts that still have to work a year later.

  4. 04

    AI features

    AI where it does concrete work: drafting a training programme, reading an ingredient list, finding what a search box cannot. In when it earns its place in the feature list, out when it does not.

Approach

How I work

Four things that stay the same from one project to the next.

  1. 01

    Every project starts from a real task

    A shopping list you use while standing in the shop. A price a customer has to find on a phone in ten seconds. A session you need to log before you forget it. The feature list follows the task, not the other way round.

  2. 02

    Built for the platform, not adapted to it

    On Android that means navigation, local reminders, background playback and offline behaviour built against the platform the apps actually run on. On the web it means markup that works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and pages that still load on a slow connection.

  3. 03

    Only the data the job needs

    Every stored field, script and cookie has to justify itself. Core use in Shopping List & Notes and WaveIQ Radio runs from data kept on the device. Online services are used where the feature needs them: AI tools, shared lists, recommendations, backup and the account-linked parts of GainsAI. Työtori is the honest exception: job searching needs an account and a server, and its own page says so.

  4. 04

    Documented, not only published

    Every app has its own privacy policy, terms, support page and data deletion instructions on this site, in Finnish and English, maintained alongside the app itself. A customer project is handed over the same way: what runs where, and what keeping it running involves.
