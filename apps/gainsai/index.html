Features

The app reads the training you logged and puts recommendations next to it, instead of only drawing charts.

Coach

What the recommendations are, and what they are not

GainsAI Coach

Coach uses the app's own backend, your current training context and deterministic coaching logic to explain program, progress and training-priority signals.

It is built to help you understand possible next actions. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian, personal trainer or other healthcare professional.

Adaptive, not absolute

Recommendations can draw attention to exercise progress, training consistency, recovery check-ins and body-trend context. They are suggestions to assess, not promises of a result.

Training decisions stay yours. Use your own judgement and seek qualified professional advice when your circumstances call for it.

Privacy

Account, data and backup

Privacy and account controls

GainsAI uses authenticated backend services for account-linked features. Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication, the backend and Google Drive are used only for the functions you choose to use.

You can delete a GainsAI account from the app. The flow covers backend and local account data, can include the GainsAI Drive backup, and attempts Firebase Authentication cleanup where possible. Deleting an account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Google Drive backup and restore

Pro includes Google Drive backup and restore. Drive is authorised separately, and backups are stored in Google Drive's app-specific storage area.

Before replacing an existing backup, the app can ask you to review recovery and account-mismatch information, so changing device or account is a decision you make with the facts in front of you.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Does GainsAI replace a healthcare or fitness professional?

No. GainsAI is a fitness and wellness tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency assistance or professional healthcare advice.

How is Pro access handled?

Purchases are made through Google Play. The app sends the purchase for secure backend verification, then shows the backend-confirmed Pro entitlement. You can restore purchases and manage a Google Play subscription through the app or Google Play.

Can I back up or restore my data?

Yes. GainsAI Pro includes Google Drive backup and restore after you authorise a Google account for Drive. The Drive connection is separate from your GainsAI sign-in.

How can I delete my account and data?

Use the in-app account deletion flow or the published deletion instructions. The app can delete account data and optionally remove the GainsAI backup from Google Drive; a subscription must be managed separately in Google Play.
