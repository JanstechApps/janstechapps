Features

Everything the week needs, in one app

The app started as a shopping list and grew into the rest of the notes people keep on their phone anyway.

  1. 01

    Lists and tasks

    Shopping lists with item editing, reordering, a purchased state, deletion and favourites. Tasks support local one-time reminders.

  2. 02

    Notes, recipes and planning

    Notes and folders, recipes saved as notes, weekly meal plans, and birthdays with local birthday reminders.

  3. 03

    Optional AI help

    AI features can generate recipes and weekly meal plans, scan ingredients, suggest recipes from what was scanned, and save the result to your notes. None of it is required for ordinary use.

  4. 04

    Shared lists when they help

    Shared shopping and task lists are optional. Sign in with Google, invite someone with a link, and see updates in real time. Local “share as copy” stays separate and needs no account.

  5. 05

    Your own copy of the data

    Local backup, export and import for local app data, plus theme options and optional biometric protection for selected views.

Local first

What works without an account

Stored on the device

The core app works locally. These are kept on the phone and stay there unless you export them or turn on an online feature:

  • Shopping lists, favourites and tasks
  • Notes, folders and recipes saved as notes
  • Birthdays and local reminders
  • Settings and local backup, export and import

Online, only where you ask for it

Two areas need the network. Signing in does not upload your local lists, notes, recipes, birthdays or reminders.

  • AI recipes, meal plans and ingredient scanning
  • Shared shopping and task lists, which are cloud data
  • Shared lists may store list metadata, items, memberships, roles, invite metadata and notification settings
