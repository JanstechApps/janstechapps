# Työtori – Legal & Support Pages

This folder holds the public legal and support pages for the Android app **Työtori**
(`com.janstech.tyotori`), served from this repository by GitHub Pages.

## Live pages
- Privacy Policy: https://janstechapps.com/legal/tyotori/
- Support: https://janstechapps.com/legal/tyotori/support.html
- Terms of Use: https://janstechapps.com/legal/tyotori/terms.html
- Delete Data: https://janstechapps.com/legal/tyotori/delete-data.html

## Sources for the content
The privacy and deletion text is written from the Työtori project's own
data-flow evidence and from the draft policy kept in that project, not from
memory. When Työtori's data handling changes, update these pages from the same
source and move the "Updated" date.

## Notes
- Support email: janstech.apps@gmail.com
- Update the "Updated" date when you change content.
- The Google Play Console privacy policy URL and the in-app privacy link are
  configured in the Työtori project, not here. If these pages are meant to be
  the canonical ones, both have to be pointed at
  `https://janstechapps.com/legal/tyotori/`.
