Support

Help with signing in, searching, application drafts, the Työtori browser and the Työtori+ subscription.

Contact

Email: janstech.apps@gmail.com

You can also send feedback from the app: Settings → Send feedback. That message includes the app version and device details, and no profile, CV, job or application content.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? Yes. Työtori keeps your profile, CV, saved jobs and applications on its backend, so sign-in is needed. You can create an account with an email address and password, or sign in with Google.
  • The password reset email did not arrive: check the spam folder and make sure you used the same address you signed up with. You can request a new link from the sign-in screen.
  • “Your free search quota is used up”: the free version includes ten successful searches per 30 days on Työmarkkinatori. The screen shows when the quota renews. Työtori+ removes the limit.
  • “In the Free version, you can save up to 10 active jobs”: the limit counts only saved jobs that do not yet have an application draft. Delete a saved job you no longer need, or create the draft, and the limit frees up.
  • A job source is greyed out or marked Työtori+: Työmarkkinatori is free. Duunitori, Jobly, Kuntarekry and Valtiolle.fi are part of Työtori+.
  • “We could not read a job posting from this link”: some employer pages require sign-in or block automated reading. Open the posting in the Työtori browser and paste the posting text manually — the draft can be created from pasted text.
  • No jobs were found: try broader keywords, add locations or the whole country, or turn on more job sources. Public job boards also return nothing for some search words.
  • The application draft could not be created: Työtori needs your name in the profile, a CV or written CV text, and the posting description. The error message names the one that is missing.
  • The application text is not quite right: it is a draft. Edit it in the app, or change the application language and create it again. Always check the facts about yourself before you send.
  • Form help does not find the form: open the employer’s actual application form first, then press “Check the form”. Sign-in pages are never analysed. If the page cannot be handled, open it in an external browser.
  • Google or Microsoft sign-in does not work in the Työtori browser: external sign-in providers are deliberately not handled inside it. Use the employer’s email sign-in or account creation if there is one, or open the form in an external browser.
  • The email draft opened without attachments: some email apps drop attachments from a draft. Attach the application letter PDF and the CV yourself, or save the attachments to your phone first and add them from there.
  • The review period counter did not move: only marking an application as sent moves it. Archiving an application that was already sent does not count it a second time, and deleting an unsent draft does not change the count.
  • I bought Työtori+ but it is not active: a purchase becomes active only after the backend has verified it with Google Play. If the purchase is pending, wait a moment and try “Restore purchase” in the Työtori+ view.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? In Google Play, from the subscription management link in the Työtori+ view. Cancelling does not delete anything you have saved.
  • How do I change the app language? Settings → Language. Työtori runs in Finnish, Swedish and English, and the language of an application document is chosen separately in the draft.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, the Android version, the app version from Settings, and what you were doing just before it happened. Do not send passwords or account credentials by email.
