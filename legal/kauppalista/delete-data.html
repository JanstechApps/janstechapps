Delete your data and account

How to remove local app data, a Firebase user account, and optional shared collaboration data.

Local app data

Most app data, such as local shopping lists, notes, favorites, and preferences, is stored locally in the app's Room database on your device. Deleting a Firebase user account does not delete this local data. To delete local app data:

Android (general steps)

  1. Open Settings
  2. Go to Apps → Shopping List & Notes
  3. Open Storage
  4. Tap Clear data (and optionally Clear cache)
  5. Optionally uninstall the app to remove it completely

Delete your Firebase user account in the app

For a Google account used with shared lists, open Settings → Account → Delete user account. Review your owned shared lists and, where other active members exist, choose either permanent deletion or ownership transfer to an eligible member.

Before the irreversible request is submitted, the app asks you to authenticate with Google again. This recent Google reauthentication confirms the account that is requesting deletion.

The app first confirms that the request was received and then shows its processing status. A completed status means cloud deletion finished. If processing fails, the request may need support follow-up; it is not treated as a completed deletion.

Shared lists and Google Sign-In data

When the account-deletion request completes, it removes the Firebase Authentication user account and the associated shared-list membership and navigation records, invitations created by that account, FCM device tokens, and shared-list notification settings. It also removes copies of the departing user's identifier from shared-list entries.

For a shared list you own, you choose before confirming whether the list is permanently deleted or its ownership is transferred to an eligible active member. A list with no other active members is permanently deleted. Transferred list content remains available to its other members.

Other users' accounts and their data are not deleted by your account-deletion request. Your own membership and profile copies are removed from shared lists that continue after an ownership transfer.

Signing out is not account deletion

Signing out stops shared-list use on this device and removes or disables its notification token where applicable. It does not delete your Firebase Authentication account, shared-list membership, or cloud data. Use the in-app account-deletion path above to request deletion.

Push notification data

Shared list push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can disable notifications from Android notification settings or from the app settings where available. Account deletion removes the FCM device tokens and shared-list notification settings associated with the deleting account after processing completes.

Exports and backups

If you use export/import backups, deleting a Firebase user account or app data does not automatically delete files you exported elsewhere. Delete those files manually from your device storage, cloud storage, or any other location where you saved them.

Request data deletion

You can also request account deletion outside the app by emailing info@janstechapps.com with the subject “Kauppalista account deletion request” and the email address of the Google account used for shared lists. This webpage does not submit an automatic deletion request.

We verify that the request concerns the account before acting and may ask for confirmation in the app with Google. Do not send your password, one-time codes, authentication tokens, shared-list contents, or backup files by email.
